What we collect and why

Enquiries by form, email or phone. When you send us an enquiry, we collect your name, phone number or email address, and your message. We use this only to reply, arrange an assessment and prepare a quote. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

Providing this data is not mandatory, but without it we cannot reply to your enquiry.

Visiting the website. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. On every visit, GitHub logs your IP address and technical browser data for security and to keep the service running. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Cookies. This website does not use cookies or any tracking or analytics tools.

Who receives your data

We never sell your data or use it for advertising. Only the providers we use to run the website and communicate with you have access to it:

  • Formspark (Trampoline Software SRL, Belgium) — receives form messages and forwards them to our email. Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google (Gmail) — the email service where we receive enquiries.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) — website hosting.

Google and GitHub also process data in the United States. Transfers take place under the safeguards in Chapter V of the GDPR (the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses).

How long we keep data

We keep enquiry data for at most 5 years from our last contact with you, after which we delete it. If you sign a contract with us, data needed for invoicing is kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Data logged by GitHub is kept by GitHub under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

At any time you can ask for:

  • access to your data and a copy of it,
  • correction of inaccurate data,
  • deletion of your data,
  • restriction of processing,
  • transfer of your data to another controller,
  • and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest.

Send your request to nikolissmilja@gmail.com. We will reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe your rights have been violated, you can lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb, azop.hr.

Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing and do not create profiles.
